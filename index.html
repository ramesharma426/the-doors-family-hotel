A Rock 'n' Roll Garden

Tucked away on Ranga Marg, The Door's Family Hotel is Hetauda's home for good food, cold drinks, and loud guitars. Lantern-lit garden paths wind past cozy cottages to an open-air dining terrace with mountain-sunset views.

Named in tribute to the legends of rock, our walls carry the spirit of The Doors, Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, and more — right down to our Rock 'n' Roll Zone and the iconic red telephone booth waiting for your photos.

Every weekend, the stage lights up with live music. On match days, the big screen takes over. Come for the food, stay for the vibe.

The Experience

What Awaits You

Cozy Cottages

Private cottages along lantern-lit garden paths — your own quiet corner for dinner with family and friends.

Live Music

Local artists and bands take our open-air stage every weekend, under the gaze of the Lizard King himself.

Match Days on the Big Screen

Football, cricket, and every big game — projected large with the crowd roaring around you.

Open-Air Dining

Wooden tables under a sheltered terrace, open to the breeze and Hetauda's finest sunsets.

Rock 'n' Roll Zone

A wall of legends — band art, classic tees, and The Doors bar celebrating the greatest era of music.

Large Parking

Spacious, hassle-free parking for bikes and cars — roll in, park easy, and head straight to your table.
